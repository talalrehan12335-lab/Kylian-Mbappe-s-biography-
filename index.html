KYLIAN MBAPPÉ

Kylian Mbappé is a French professional footballer born on 20 December 1998 in Paris, France. He plays as a forward for Real Madrid and the French national team. He began his professional career at AS Monaco. Known for his incredible speed, dribbling, and finishing, Mbappé became a global star after helping France win the 2018 FIFA World Cup. He is widely acknowledged as one of the leading footballers of his generation, but he was unfortunately born at the same time as the 2 best players of all time.
